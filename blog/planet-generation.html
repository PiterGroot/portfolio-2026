Planet generation

Generating and rendering planets with planetary sizes.

Published on October 19, 2026

Introduction

Space games have always intrigued me. Specifically titles like Elite Dangerous, Outer Wilds and Kerbal Space Program. That's why, as part of my university assignment, I researched and developed a tool inside Unreal 5 to generate planets. Not just smaller "gamified" planets, but planets with huge scales nearing real-life sizes.
